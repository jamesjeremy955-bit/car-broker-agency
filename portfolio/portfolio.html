Our Focus • Why Enthusiast-Spec Assets Command a Premium

Manual-Transmission, Collectible & Timeless Driver’s Cars.

1994 Dodge Viper RT/10 · $35,251

Scher Masella Motorcars specializes in manual-transmission, analog, and rare enthusiast-spec vehicles—timeless machines that hold enduring value.

Every deal is unique. Rather than applying a rigid, one-size-fits-all template, we take the time to curate each vehicle’s preparation, documentation, and narrative around what makes that specific asset special—leveraging both international auction platforms and our private client network to achieve the highest possible return.

01

Enduring, Enthusiast-Led Value

Analog drivetrains, factory specifications and honest provenance are finite. Informed enthusiasts pay for them, and the best examples keep their appeal long after the hammer falls.

02

Curated, Never Templated

No two cars are prepared alike. Photography, documentation, option decoding and storytelling are built around the specific history and specification that matter most for that exact vehicle.

03

Two Routes to Market

International auction platforms create competitive price discovery; our private client network provides a direct path when the right buyer is already known—or when an auction falls just short.

Advisory Approach

How we maximize your return at every stage of the auction.

01

Forensic Photo & Video Suite

Comprehensive high-resolution photography, engine bay and mechanical detail documentation, and documented cold starts, delivering full cosmetic transparency so condition is established before bidders have to ask.

02

Collector Storytelling

Listings written for the people who actually bid: provenance, specification, ownership history and what makes the car desirable, presented to the standard enthusiast buyers expect.

03

24/7 Auction-Week Moderation

Comments decide a seven-day auction. Questions receive fast, factual and transparent answers, skeptics receive documentation, and the seller’s voice stays consistent through the final bid.

04

Settlement & White-Glove Hand-off

Transaction settlement is tailored to client preference—whether coordinated via verified wire, certified bank instrument, or attorney escrow—followed by insured nationwide transport coordination to ensure secure completion.
